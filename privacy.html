Agro AI — Privacy Policy

Agro AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides agricultural advisory tools, AI leaf disease diagnostics, weather and mandi market insights, farm-to-market logistics, and the Agro AI Mart farm-to-consumer marketplace. This Privacy Policy describes what personal data we collect, how it is used, how it is protected, and how you can access or delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data: Name, phone number, email address, preferred language, and role (farmer, seller, driver, or customer) when you sign in via Supabase Auth or Google Sign-In.
  • Farm & Seller Verification Data: Farm name, village, district, state, serviceable PIN codes, and farmer-submitted land record reference (e.g., Pahani / RTC / 7-12 extract) when applying to sell on Agro AI Mart.
  • Camera & Crop Images: Photos of crop leaves or produce that you explicitly capture or upload for AI disease diagnosis or marketplace listings.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise location (when permitted by you) and postal PIN code to display local weather advisories, nearby APMC mandi prices, logistics trips, and serviceable Mart farms.
  • Voice Input: Audio spoken into the Kisan Voice Assistant when you actively tap the microphone button.
  • Orders & Subscription Records: Marketplace order items, delivery address, fulfilment status, and subscription payment references (processed via Razorpay; Agro AI does not store raw card numbers or UPI PINs).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and enforce role-based access controls.
  • To analyze uploaded crop leaf photos using AI vision models and provide pathology treatment guidance.
  • To connect verified local farmers with nearby consumers on Agro AI Mart and coordinate farm-gate pickup or delivery.
  • To provide localized weather alerts, government scheme eligibility matching, and APMC mandi price trends.
  • To maintain security audit logs, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable Indian laws (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data or use third-party behavioral advertising SDKs. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure providers required to operate the service:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database infrastructure.
  • Google Gemini / OpenAI API: Crop leaf image diagnosis and agricultural advisory generation when configured on the server.
  • Razorpay: Subscription payment order processing and cryptographic verification.
  • Fulfilment Counterparties: When you place an order on Agro AI Mart or book a logistics trip, your delivery address and contact phone number are shared only with the fulfilling verified farmer or assigned driver.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may delete your account and personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Account → Delete My Account & Personal Data inside the Agro AI app.
  • Web Deletion Portal: Visit /delete-account to delete your account immediately or submit a deletion request without reinstalling the app.

When you delete your account, your profile credentials, farms, crop diagnosis history, chat history, and saved items are permanently deleted, and your account is immediately deactivated. Completed marketplace order invoices and financial audit logs are anonymized and retained only as required for tax, accounting, and dispute-resolution compliance.
