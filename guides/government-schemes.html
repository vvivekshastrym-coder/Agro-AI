Guide to PM-KISAN, FRUITS & Krishi Subsidies | Agro AI

Step-by-step instructions on applying for PM-KISAN installments, Karnataka FRUITS registration, KCC low-interest loans, and PMFBY insurance.

FRUITS Farmer Registration (Karnataka)

FRUITS provides a unique Farmer ID linked to Bhoomi RTC land survey records, enabling direct subsidy transfers without paperwork.

PM-KISAN e-KYC & Land Seeding

Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your active bank account (NPCI DBT enabled) and land records are seeded to receive the ₹6,000 annual benefit.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Subsidized Loans

KCC offers crop loans at an effective 4% interest rate with prompt repayment. Apply through your local cooperative or nationalized bank branch.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Insure non-preventable natural risk (drought, flood, unseasonal pest attack) before the cut-off date with subsidized 1.5% to 2% farmer premiums.
