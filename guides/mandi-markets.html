Reading Min, Max, and Modal Prices

The modal price represents the rate at which most lots traded that day, making it more useful than extreme high or low quotes.

Grading & Moisture Impact

Clean, uniform, properly dried produce consistently fetches closer to the upper mandi band than unsorted lots.

Comparing Nearby Markets

Factor in transport cost, loading fees, and transit weight loss before choosing a distant mandi over a local APMC yard.
