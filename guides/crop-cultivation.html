Seed Selection & Seedbed Preparation

Use certified, region-adapted varieties and prepare a well-drained seedbed before monsoon onset or Rabi sowing.

Critical Irrigation Stages

Prioritize moisture during germination, flowering, and grain or fruit filling while avoiding waterlogging.

Integrated Weed & Pest Management

Timely inter-cultivation in the first 30 days after sowing reduces competition for water and nutrients.
