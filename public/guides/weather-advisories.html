Master microclimate weather management: identify safe spray windows, humidity disease triggers, and rain forecast tracking for harvesting.

Planning Safe Spray Windows

Avoid foliar spraying when wind speeds are high or rain is forecast within 4 to 6 hours to prevent wash-off and drift.

Humidity & Disease Risk

Extended high relative humidity paired with moderate temperatures increases fungal spore germination; inspect lower leaves after humid nights.

Harvest & Drying Decisions

Schedule harvesting and sun-drying during dry multi-day windows to protect grain quality and reduce post-harvest spoilage.
