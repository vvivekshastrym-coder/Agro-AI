Crop Disease & Leaf Scouting Field Guide | Agro AI

Learn how to identify early leaf spots, powdery mildew, rusts, and nutrient deficiencies with preventative organic and chemical field remedies.

Why Early Leaf Scouting Matters

Walking fields twice a week helps spot fungal lesions, powdery mildew, and pest feeding before symptoms spread across the whole plot.

How to Take a Clear Diagnostic Photo

Capture a well-lit, focused photo of a single affected leaf against a plain background, avoiding harsh shadows or motion blur.

Preventive Field Hygiene

Remove severely infected plant debris, avoid overhead irrigation late in the evening, and rotate non-host crops between seasons.
