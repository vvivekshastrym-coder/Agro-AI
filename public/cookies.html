Agro AI — Cookie & Consent Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Essential Cookies Only by Default

Agro AI uses strictly necessary cookies and local tokens for authentication, CSRF/security protection, and remembering your privacy preferences. Non-essential analytics and marketing cookies are disabled by default until you explicitly opt in.

2. Granular Controls & Withdrawal

  • Essential: Always active for login sessions and security.
  • Analytics (Optional): Disabled by default; never includes session replay or keystroke capture.
  • Marketing (Optional): Disabled by default; you can withdraw consent at any time in Account → Privacy & Cookie Settings.
